The first 1.0 release brings your headset controls into a lightweight, customizable
Windows overlay that works without Audeze App running.

- **Your sound at a glance.** See game/chat balance as you turn the headset wheel,
  and adjust sidetone, noise cancellation and microphone noise removal from the overlay.
- **Make it fit your screen.** Rearrange and resize cards, choose a compact mix
  slider, and adjust transparency, blur and text size. Keep empty space when you
  want it, or let the layout close the gaps.
- **Hear EQ changes as you make them.** Tune ten bands live, pin favorite presets,
  and use the mini EQ editor inside a larger card. Save keeps your changes;
  Discard restores the curve from before editing.
- **Take your presets with you.** Import and export EQ profiles, keep a local
  preset library, and save a new copy without overwriting the original.
- **Keep your game/chat balance when connecting a cable.** The overlay restores
  the previous mix after a wired/wireless transition. This was tested in both
  directions on Maxwell 2 ANC; a brief firmware reset may still be audible.
- **See the status that matters.** An optional fixed status row shows the headset
  model, connection, battery and microphone state.
- **Choose when the overlay appears.** Show only the card that changed, set popup
  timing, and record your preferred shortcut. Unfinished shortcut recordings now
  prompt you to save or discard before leaving the page.
- **A simpler installation.** Windows lists the app as **Audeze Overlay**, and
  existing installations can be upgraded with the new installer.

Maxwell 2 ANC is the hardware-tested model. Additional Maxwell profiles remain
experimental, and custom EQ editing on non-ANC models is disabled. See the
[device coverage and limitations](https://github.com/temdah/AudezeOverlay/blob/v1.0.0/docs/DEVICE_PROTOCOL.md).
