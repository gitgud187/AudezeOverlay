- **More reliable launch with Windows.** Startup now follows the installed app
  instead of leaving a shortcut to an old portable or development copy. Opening
  the app repairs an enabled startup entry, and installer upgrades update its path.
- **Clearer connection status.** When the dongle is present but headset controls
  stop replying, the overlay shows **No data** and retries without repeatedly
  switching between an empty headset display and “Headset disconnected.” Stale
  values are cleared until fresh readings arrive.
- **Same polling speed.** The mix polling target and background query intervals
  are unchanged. Startup repair runs once when opening the app.

This update improves startup and how control-channel interruptions are displayed.
It does not prevent a headset or dongle from losing its wireless connection.
